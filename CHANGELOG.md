# Changelog

## 0.1.0

- First release.
- Zoom out further: up to 16 m while walking, 40 m while steering a ship and 24 m while riding (vanilla 8 m, 16 m on ships). Each has its own limits in the config and remembers its own zoom.
- Zoom in past the closest distance for a first-person view, with your body hidden so it doesn't block the view.
- While steering a ship or riding, press `C` to switch the camera between following you and following the centre of the ship or mount. While doing either, `C` no longer toggles walking.
